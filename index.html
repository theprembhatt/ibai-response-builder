Tell IRDAI what its distribution paper means for your work

0 of 9 answered

This is voluntary and personal. IRDAI's consultation paper proposes caps on what insurers may spend on distribution and on what brokers are paid. Those caps land on people: the staff who place cover, service policies and get claims paid. If you work in insurance broking and want IRDAI to hear what that means in practice, this page helps you write a short response in your own name, in your personal capacity. Nine of the 32 questions touch your work; two or three points are ticked under each and the selection and wording differ from person to person, so no two responses read alike. Identical answers would not truly reflect your individual feedback for the Authority to consider. Untick what you do not agree with, and add a line or two of your own experience where a question asks for it, because that is what IRDAI cannot get from anyone else. Nobody at your firm should ask to see what you send, and this page keeps nothing except on your own phone. This page was prepared by the Insurance Brokers Association of India, the body that represents broking firms; the response you build is yours alone. Then press Build my response.

About you

FileHow to send this to IRDAI (by )read first

IRDAI accepts responses in three ways. Use one of them, not more, or the submission may be counted twice.

1. Web form at iib.gov.in/dr. The form asks for each question separately: open your response below and use the Copy button beside each question to paste that answer into the matching box. Leave the boxes for questions you did not answer empty.

2. Excel template, downloaded from the same portal under Section 17, filled in and uploaded there. Copy each answer into the row for that question.

3. Email the completed Excel template to drfeedback@iib.gov.in with the subject line Reforms for Recalibrating Economics of Insurance Distribution .

Each answer is limited to 250 words (Q32: 500); the counts shown below are already within those limits. Only the 32 answers can be uploaded. None of your personal details is written into them, and no answer says where you work; your name and details appear only in the opening paragraph and signature when you copy the whole response. Leave the questions you did not answer blank. You file this yourself, from your own email or the portal; nobody at your firm needs to see it.
